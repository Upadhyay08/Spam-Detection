The workflow

From raw SMS to prediction.

The project starts with labelled SMS text, separates message and label columns, creates a stratified train/test split, converts text to TF-IDF features, and fits Logistic Regression.

04 · ClassifierLogistic Regression → ham / spam

What it means

On this test split, every message predicted as spam was actually spam, giving 100% spam precision. The model caught 119 of 149 actual spam messages, giving 79.87% spam recall.

The notebook also examines incorrect predictions and individual TF-IDF feature contributions to understand why the Logistic Regression model moves toward spam or ham.
Explainability

It doesn't just say “spam”.

The notebook goes one level deeper: it transforms a message with TF-IDF, reads the Logistic Regression weights, calculates feature contributions, and inspects the decision score.

TF-IDF

Words are converted into weighted numerical features based on their importance in the SMS corpus.

Weight

Logistic Regression assigns learned positive or negative weights to vocabulary features.

Contribution

TF-IDF value × model weight shows how individual words push the decision.

Score

The decision function provides the model's raw classification score before probability conversion.
